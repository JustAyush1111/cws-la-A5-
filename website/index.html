A Taste of New York

FIRST STOP · NEW YORK

Statue of Liberty

The Story Behind the Statue

A gift of friendship

The Statue of Liberty was gifted by France to the United States as a symbol of freedom and friendship.

  • Located on Liberty Island, New York Harbor.
  • Dedicated on October 28, 1886.
  • Designed by Frédéric Auguste Bartholdi.
  • Its torch represents enlightenment.
SECOND STOP · NEW YORK

Times Square

Interesting Times Square Facts

  • Times Square is located in Midtown Manhattan.
  • It is famous for its giant digital billboards.
  • The famous New Year's Eve Ball Drop began in 1907.
  • Broadway theatres are nearby.
THIRD STOP · NEW YORK

Brooklyn Bridge

The Story Behind the Bridge

The Brooklyn Bridge connects Lower Manhattan and Brooklyn Heights across the East River.

  • Construction began in 1869 and was completed in 1883.
  • It was the longest suspension bridge in the world when it opened.
  • It was designed by John A. Roebling.
  • It includes a pedestrian promenade and a protected bicycle path.
Source: NYC Department of Transportation
